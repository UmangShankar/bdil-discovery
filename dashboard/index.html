Import YouTube report

Select Table data.csv from the downloaded ZIP. Enter the date range used for the report. Existing videos are updated by video ID; your labels and 24-hour snapshots are kept. New videos are unclassified: edit each to set Short or Long.

Import TikTok content

Choose Content.csv from your TikTok Content ZIP. The file has month and day but no year: enter the year for both capture and post dates. Files spanning a year boundary cannot be imported yet. Video IDs prevent duplicates; existing tags and snapshots are kept.
